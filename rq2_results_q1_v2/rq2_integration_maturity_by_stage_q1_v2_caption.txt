Figure 4. Integration maturity across clinical stages. Bars show the within-stage distribution of Research only, Proposed only, and Evaluated AI use among all 428 included studies. Proposed only denotes a stated clinical or translational application without observed workflow evaluation, whereas Evaluated AI use denotes point-of-use, intervention, or care-delivery evaluation in the assessed sources.
